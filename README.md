# Youth Innovative Entrepreneurship Survey Data

## Overview
This repository contains the results of a sociological survey conducted to assess the potential for the development of youth innovative entrepreneurship in Kazakhstan. The data can be used for statistical analysis, modeling, and policy research.

## Objective
The primary aim of the survey is to identify factors and barriers affecting the growth of innovative entrepreneurial activity among young people (aged 18–35) in Kazakhstan.

## Dataset Content
- **Number of questions:** 16  
- **Number of respondents:** 601  
- **Format:** CSV (comma-separated values)

Each row in `data/raw/survey_results.csv` represents one respondent. Columns correspond to survey questions (demographics, attitudes, perceived obstacles, etc.).
